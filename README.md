# Gambito

**Chess training and analysis for Windows.** Solve puzzles at your level, learn tactics, mates and endgames with short
lessons, play engines at any strength, and see where your games were won or lost, move by move.

> Gambito is a study tool. Using an engine during a game you are playing (online, rated, tournaments) breaks the
> rules of every chess site. Imported games are finished games only.

## Features

- **Play** against an engine from Elo 1000 to 2600, or both sides yourself, with the 3 best moves shown and explained
  in plain words.
- **Game review** after every game: accuracy, a chart of the evaluation with the big moments marked, each move rated
  from brilliant to blunder, an estimated rating for the game, accuracy in the opening, middlegame and endgame, and a
  short summary of where the game turned. Games you play against the engine are kept to review later.
- **Puzzles**: about 10,000 puzzles picked around your rating, with hints, solutions and a filter by theme.
- **Lessons**: 17 short lessons on tactics, mates, endgames and openings, each with examples and practice.
- **Games**: 11 short games for board vision and memory: recall the pawns, follow a knight's route, play blindfold,
  spot the check, a mate sprint, spot what changed, legal or illegal, a hanging-piece cash-out, safe squares, colour
  queens and pairs.
- **Import games** from your chess.com or Lichess profile, from any PGN, or from a **screenshot** of a board (just press `Ctrl+V`).
- **7 engines** to choose from (Stockfish, Berserk, Obsidian, Koivisto, Alexandria, Caissa, Viridithas), plus any UCI engine.
- **Feels like a real board**: move sounds, five board colours, promotion to any piece, drawing arrows and squares
  with the right mouse button, and short, smooth animations you can turn off (they also follow Windows' own setting).
- **English and Portuguese**, light on your PC, and it updates itself.

## Install

Download **`Gambito-Setup.exe`** from [Releases](../../releases) and run it. It installs for your user only (no
administrator rights) and lets you pick the language, the engines and the folder. Engines are downloaded from their
official pages and checked before they are installed. Uninstall from Windows "Installed apps".

## Privacy

No account and no tracking. Gambito works offline, except for:
- downloading the engines during installation;
- looking up your chess.com or Lichess games, only when you search (just the username is sent);
- checking GitHub for a new version once a day (can be turned off in Settings).

Your progress stays on your PC.
